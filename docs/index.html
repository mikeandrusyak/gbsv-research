GBSV — Signal & Image Processing Research

Two mini-challenges on the fundamentals of signal and image processing: analyzing a synthetic train-bogie vibration signal, and applying classical image-processing methods to my own photos of Swiss subjects.

Python NumPy SciPy scikit-image Signal Processing Image Processing

Overview

Project Overview

University coursework at FHNW (Switzerland) for the module "Grundlagen der Bild- und Signalverarbeitung" (fundamentals of image and signal processing). Each of the six tasks is a self-contained experiment documented day by day: data & domain, methodological design, implementation, evaluation, and critical reflection.

Mini-Challenge 1 — Signals

A synthetic 1D vibration signal simulating a defective train-bogie bearing (inspired by a Kaggle predictive-maintenance dataset) is generated with a reproducible script, then analyzed on a fixed 1.0 s window at 575 Hz.

Mini-Challenge 2 — Images

Classical image processing applied to real photographs: road signs in Geneva, Swiss coins, and the eyes of Emmental cheese — augmentation, template-based pattern detection, and segmentation.

What I Did

  • Built a reproducible synthetic-signal generator with metadata and event timestamps for traceability
  • Demonstrated the sampling theorem, aliasing, and reconstruction on the vibration signal
  • Located defect events with cross-correlation against a known impulse pattern
  • Modeled sensor smearing with convolution and recovered events via deconvolution
  • Designed an augmentation pipeline and verified each transform on the Geneva road-signs photo
  • Detected and classified Swiss coin denominations with template matching
  • Segmented Emmental cheese eyes, including lighting correction for illuminated rims
  • Evaluated every method quantitatively and reflected on its limitations
